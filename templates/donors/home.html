Awareness & Health

Why Your Donation Truly Matters

Blood cannot be manufactured synthetically in laboratories. It can only come as a generous gift from caring human beings like you.

Saves Emergency Patients

Accident victims, surgical patients, mothers experiencing childbirth complications, and cancer patients undergoing chemotherapy require urgent transfusions every single day.

Health Benefits for Donors

Regular blood donation stimulates bone marrow to produce fresh blood cells, helps regulate body iron levels, and includes a preliminary checkup of blood pressure, pulse, and hemoglobin.

Hyper-Local Community Help

With Jeevadhara's city-level filtering, hospitals and families can locate registered nearby donors in minutes without relying on panicked social media forwards.

Simple & Safe

How Blood Donation Works

The entire donation process takes less than an hour, with the actual blood draw taking only 8 to 10 minutes.

1
Register Online

Fill out your contact details, blood group, city, and donation willingness on Jeevadhara.

2
Health Screening

At the donation center, medical staff checks temperature, pulse, hemoglobin, and blood pressure.

3
Safe Donation

350–450 ml of blood is collected using single-use, sterile, vacuum-sealed equipment.

4
Rest & Refreshment

Enjoy light juice and snacks for 10-15 minutes before carrying on with your regular day.

Medical Eligibility

Are You Eligible to Donate?

Before registering, ensure that you meet standard health criteria formulated by national blood transfusion councils.

Age Limit:
You must be between 18 and 65 years of age.
Minimum Body Weight:
Minimum weight of 45 kg (50 kg recommended for 450ml collections).
Hemoglobin Level:
Not less than 12.5 g/dL.
Donation Interval:
At least 90 days (3 months) since your last whole blood donation.
